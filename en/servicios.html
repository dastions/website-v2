Metrology · Calibration and verification

ENAC-accredited calibration and verification

We calibrate and verify your weighing instruments through an ENAC-accredited partner laboratory, with metrological traceability and an official certificate. We guarantee the accuracy of your scales and compliance with legal metrology.

  • Calibration of scales, standard weights and weighing instruments.
  • Metrological verification for instruments in regulated use.
  • ENAC-accredited certificate of calibration and verification.
  • Traceability to the International System of Units (SI).
  • Compliance with legal metrology and current regulations.
