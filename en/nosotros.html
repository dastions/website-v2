Who we are

A family company that evolves with the industry

Dastions is born from a long family tradition in industrial weighing, with the mission of taking the sector into the digital age.

Four generations of hands-on knowledge, now turned into software, IoT and artificial intelligence for manufacturers, installers and clients all over the world.

Track record

Key milestones

1924

Start in the sector

Antonio Collado González begins his career at AVERY, the leading English industrial weighing company. The first generation of a long family tradition.

1987

Foundation of Básculas Mauri

The family company is born, working with well-known brands such as MOBBA, EPELSA, PIBERNAT and METTLER TOLEDO. Consolidation and diversification of services led by German Mauri.

2022

Birth of Dastions

Oriol Mauri leads the fourth generation with a focus on IoT, software and technological innovation: Dastions, the digital evolution of weighing.
